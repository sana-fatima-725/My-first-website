Return Policy

⏰ Return Window

You must request a return within 7 days of delivery. Returns will not be accepted after 7 days.

✅ Return Conditions

Product must be in its original packaging. It should be unused and free from any damage.

❌ Non-Returnable

Returns will not be accepted if the product has been used, packaging is opened, or damage is caused by the customer.

💰 Refund

Once the return is approved, the refund will be credited to JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or bank within 3–5 working days.

1

Send a Message on WhatsApp

Message 03054603311 with your Order ID and reason for return.

2

Send Photos of the Product

Send clear photos of the product on WhatsApp so we can verify.

3

Send the Parcel After Approval

Once we approve, send the parcel to our address by courier.

Warranty Policy

🎧 Earphones & Earbuds

3 months warranty. Manufacturing defects are covered. Physical damage is not covered.

⚡ Chargers & Cables

1 month warranty. If the charger stops working or doesn't charge, we will replace it.

🔋 Power Banks

3 months warranty. Battery drain or charging issues are covered.

📱 Covers & Screen Guards

If damaged during delivery, you get a replacement. Physical damage after delivery is not covered.

Want to claim warranty? Take a video or photo of the product showing the problem clearly and send it to us on WhatsApp. We will respond within 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions
Are all products 100% original?+
Yes, Ahsan Mobiles only sells 100% original products. Every item is verified before stocking. No replicas or fake products here.
Is Cash on Delivery available?+
Yes! Cash on Delivery is available across Pakistan. Pay cash upon delivery and receive your product.
How do I cancel an order?+
If the parcel hasn't been dispatched, message us on WhatsApp and we'll cancel it. Once dispatched, cancellation isn't possible.
What if I receive the wrong product?+
Send us a photo on WhatsApp immediately. If it's our mistake, we'll provide a free replacement or full refund. No extra charges.
Where do I use a discount code?+
Mention your discount code on WhatsApp when placing your order. Use code AHSAN20 to get 20% off on your first order.
Do you deliver outside Pakistan?+
Currently, we only deliver within Pakistan. For international shipping, contact us on WhatsApp.
Is payment secure?+
Absolutely. We accept JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Bank Transfer — all secure platforms. COD option is also available if you prefer not to pay online.
